The many Calcuttas

A city in layers

Every building the city has bothered to protect, from Job Charnock’s burial ground to the Art Deco cinemas of Esplanade — plotted where it stands.

Where this comes from

Sites are drawn from the Kolkata Municipal Corporation’s graded list of heritage buildings, the Archaeological Survey of India’s monuments of national importance, the West Bengal Heritage Commission’s declared sites and the railway heritage inventory — cross-referenced against Wikidata for coordinates and English Wikipedia for the descriptions.

Coverage runs to roughly 25 km from Lal Dighi, which takes in Howrah, Baranagar, Dakshineswar, Salt Lake, Behala, Serampore and Barrackpore. Construction dates are only shown where a source records one; for a large part of the graded list nobody ever wrote the year down.
